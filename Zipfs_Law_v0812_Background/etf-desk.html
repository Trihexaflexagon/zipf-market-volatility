METHODS / 01

Calculation rules

Prices. The page automatically downloads a static snapshot of daily ETF adjusted closes from a third-party Yahoo Finance mirror, through August 11, 2026. It tries GitHub and a CDN mirror. Once retrieved, the file is saved in browser storage if available, so a later network failure does not require an upload. These prices have not been independently audited. You may optionally replace them with your own CSV.

Timing. Signals are calculated at the previous trading session’s close. Orders are assumed filled at the selected rebalance day’s closing price; the new weights affect the next session’s return. This one-session delay avoids forming a signal from the same closing price used for execution. Actual fills can differ.

Selection. Momentum uses price at t minus the skip interval divided by price at t minus lookback, minus one. Reversal ranks the same score ascending. Low volatility ranks by daily standard deviation over the prior 63 sessions. Equal weight holds the eligible selected universe. Assets failing an enabled SMA filter are excluded, with unused allocation held as noninterest-bearing cash.

Execution. Holdings drift daily with returns. At rebalance, one-way turnover is half the sum of absolute asset and cash weight changes. Estimated trading cost is assessed against the full gross dollar amount bought and sold, and is deducted from NAV. Fractional shares, taxes, market impact, liquidity constraints and funding costs are omitted. Buy-and-hold benchmark excludes trading costs.

Scope. This is a historical ETF research exercise, not a stock-level value or ROE factor test. The ETF universe is selected with hindsight; assets with missing earlier history are ineligible until enough observations exist. Historical patterns may not repeat. This tool is not investment advice.
